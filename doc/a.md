1. Introduction
   FarCal (Fare Calculator) is a web, mobile, and progressive web application (PWA) that helps estimate travel costs based on route distance, vehicle type, and contextual constraints such as time of day, weather, and traffic conditions (where available). This Privacy Notice describes what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices you have.
2. Who we are (Data Controller)
   FarCal is provided by Yowyob Inc. Ltd ("Yowyob", "we", "us"). For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, please contact us via our official contact page: https://yowyob.com/contact.
3. Scope
   This Privacy Notice applies to information processed in connection with FarCal, including our websites, apps/PWA, and related services. It does not cover third-party websites, apps, or services that may be linked from FarCal or used to provide certain functions (for example, mapping, weather, or traffic data providers).
4. Information we collect
   4.1 Information you provide
   • Trip inputs and preferences (for example: pickup/starting area, destination area, vehicle type, optional constraints).
   • Feedback and support communications (for example: messages you send through contact forms or in-app support).
   • If accounts are enabled: registration information such as name, email address, or phone number (as applicable).
   4.2 Information collected automatically
   • Device and technical information (for example: device model, operating system version, language, browser type, app version).
   • Identifiers and logs (for example: IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device identifiers, timestamps, diagnostics, crash data, performance metrics).
   • Usage information (for example: pages/screens viewed, feature interactions, approximate trip characteristics, and session events).
   • Cookies and similar technologies (for example: cookies, local storage, SDK identifiers) used for authentication (if applicable), security, analytics, and advertising.
   4.3 Location information (geolocation)
   If you enable location services on your device, FarCal may collect and process precise or approximate geolocation data to calculate routes, estimate distance, improve contextual accuracy, and enhance user experience. You can disable location permissions at any time in your device settings, but some features may be unavailable or less accurate.
   4.4 Information from third parties
   • Mapping and routing data (for example: base maps, route geometry, distance estimation).
   • Weather and traffic context (where available) used to refine estimates.
   • Advertising and analytics partners' event data (for example: ad impressions, clicks, conversions) subject to consent and applicable law.
5. How we use information
   We use information for the following purposes:
   • Provide and operate FarCal (for example: compute fare estimates, display results, maintain core functionality).
   • Improve and develop the Service (for example: model calibration, usability improvements, feature development).
   • Maintain security and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
   • Monitor reliability and performance (for example: crash reporting and diagnostics).
   • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
   • Deliver and measure advertising, including reporting to local business advertisers and partners.
   • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.
6. Legal bases for processing
   Where applicable data protection laws require a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following:
   • Consent (for example: precise location access; certain cookies and personalized advertising where required).
   • Contract / service necessity (for example: processing trip inputs to generate estimates).
   • Legitimate interests (for example: improving FarCal, ensuring security, preventing fraud, measuring and reporting ad performance), balanced against your rights.
   • Legal obligation (for example: responding to lawful requests or complying with applicable regulations).
   You can withdraw consent at any time where consent is the basis (for example by disabling location permissions or changing cookie preferences), without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.
7. Advertising, cookies, and similar technologies
   FarCal may be funded by advertising from local businesses and partners. We may use cookies, SDKs, and similar technologies to:
   • Serve contextual or personalized ads (where permitted and based on your settings/consent).
   • Limit ad frequency and prevent fraud.
   • Measure ad performance (for example: impressions, clicks, conversions).
   • Understand usage patterns and improve the Service.
   You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where provided, through in-app consent or privacy settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain functions.
8. How we share information
   We share information only as needed to operate and improve FarCal, and in accordance with this Privacy Notice:
   • Service providers (processors) that host data, provide analytics, support, infrastructure, and security services.
   • Advertising partners to deliver ads and measure performance, subject to consent and applicable law.
   • Business transfers (for example: merger, acquisition, or sale of assets) where permitted by law.
   • Legal reasons (for example: responding to lawful requests, protecting rights, safety, and security).

9. International data transfers
   Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including where our service providers operate. Where required, we implement appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers, consistent with applicable law.
10. Data retention
    We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described in this notice, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.     FarCal (Fare Calculator) est une application web, mobile et progressive (PWA) qui aide à estimer le coût d'un trajet en fonction de la distance, du type de véhicule et de contraintes contextuelles telles que l'heure (jour/nuit), la météo et l'état de la circulation (lorsque ces informations sont disponibles).
